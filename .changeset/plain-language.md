**`grilling`** writes in plain words, keeps each question to three short lines with a one-line recommendation, reads what the repo already decided before asking, and reopens a decision only when something new bears on it. Answering a round with **go** accepts the remaining recommendations and ends the session (a `go` checkbox in `interactive-form`). **ADRs** get plain titles that name the concrete thing, and an optional **Revisit if** line; meeting it reopens the ADR like a reversal. **`setup-matt-pocock-skills`** seeds a `### Writing` house convention: re-run it in set-up repos.
